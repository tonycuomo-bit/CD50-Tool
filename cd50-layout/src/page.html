2 · Site view

Showing a sample lot. Send a sketch back from Aerial Site Sketch, import a Google Earth image (drop it here or on the plan, with its KMZ for the scale), or load or paste (Ctrl+V) a screenshot of the overhead view, crop to the work area, then set the scale from a known dimension such as a 9 ft stall.
Saving a view from Google Earth Pro
  1. Zoom to the site, then press U (straight down) and N (north up).
  2. Add a placemark on the site (Add › Placemark). Optionally add a path (Add › Path) along a long straight edge, such as a curb line or building wall. With a path the scale is exact.
  3. File › Save › Save Image, at the highest resolution.
  4. Select the placemark and the path in the Places list (put them in one folder), then right-click › Save Place As (KMZ or KML).
  5. Here, press Import Google Earth and pick both files. With a path, click its first point and then its last point on the image.
Google Earth imagery is for preliminary, reference layouts; check Google's terms before issuing drawings traced from it.

3 · Equipment

Drag equipment to move it. Select it to rotate. Pads are hatched; dashed outlines are working clearances.

4 · Parking, ADA, signs and landscaping

New stalls, signs and plants drop at the centre of the view. Drag to place, select to rotate (R), change type, or delete (Delete key). Plants are generic (species TBD) and print on the L-101 landscape plan.

5 · Conduit schedule

Click a conduit to add a bend point, drag a point to reroute, double-click a point to remove it. Lengths are center to center on plan, before stub-ups.
